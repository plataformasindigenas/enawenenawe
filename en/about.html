Enawenê-Nawê

Digital platform for documentation and revitalization of the Enawenê-Nawê language and culture

Mission

This platform is an initiative of the Indigenous Languages Platform dedicated to the documentation, preservation, and revitalization of the language and culture of the Enawenê-Nawê people, speakers of an Arawak family language in northwestern Mato Grosso, Brazil.

Our goal is to make linguistic, ethnographic, and cultural materials available in accessible digital formats, contributing to the strengthening of Enawenê-Nawê identity and to research on Brazilian indigenous languages.

Team & Partners

This project is developed in collaboration with:

Unifacc — University center, institutional partner
Salesian Mission of Mato Grosso — Historic partnership with indigenous peoples of the region
Uppsala University — Support in computational linguistics and technology
