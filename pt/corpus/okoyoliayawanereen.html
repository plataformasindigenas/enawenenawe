Okoyoli Ayawanere En

Okoyoli e Ayawanere Ayawanere etani Okoyoli, Ayawanere ameratahana holi owiro heye takwa, enewalo Lolahanero,Lolakate esokwate ayawane, holi Ayawanere heye awalo Lolahanero, holi nanita Ayawanere ehasakalone, Lolahanero tasawati wakoyoa holi, Lolahanero aikialatene: Hala enawenero xala nato laka, nexikere wayakanetali hanolokwahena tako Awa akotekase hetalase tana, lolahanero nato nane teta nane, Ayawanere aikwalatamane: Mahia xakaliwa, Awerata heye, awali xinakaha kakwa, holidema Lolahanero, hehe newa, awa waita hexota hetalase heyotitira natoweta waita noweta hetase, awa akotekase hetalase tana newa eheye. Hehe newa Ayawanere eheye, lolahanere heye, holi akoyona ekiakwa Ayawanere masemanere hatalase yakoli eyota heu,heu,heu mahia totaliwala etalase, owahakasena netali, holi Lolahanero otokowe eheye etine kaliyane taololi newa etalase, holi Lolahanero nowa eheye: haita kera nowe heheye, eheta masemanera kaxali hixo kali, Natoweta waita nowetene noheti nowekera heheye, hetalase etine hexowa lalohi hesakwa laka hehenesekwaneta hala hetalse hakolata enako, holidema lalahe sakwa lolate towa hatalase akolata enako, akolatene toheta hotaketikwa, mahia hakolako wala exowata towane eyaokwa honako exowata towane, holidema eyaokwa honako taetã, Ayawanere eraneto, exeneto kali enewaloha, Lololawa, Lololawalo, Waikwadawalo, Enodawalo, Axodawalo, Oherolawa, yaliyalisenero. Holi kadene atahana Ayawanere owana, holi owana hatalase alikiata akoyonane, hataha, akoyoa, hataha akoyoa kali, menaneta akoyakotane anolotitera hatalase, holi enetane kañakakena. Heheta heraneto kakwa ekiata nowa heye, hala nowa heye, holidewa enetani Okoyoli awalolotene wayone, hala newa enetane Okoyoli, kalitasenane newa, ekialata nane enowana newa, aotahanane xexase kewalixa, atahana enowa, holi Ayawanere asewene hatalase alikiatama yakoli enowana, holi noxe aokwa enaita, holi heno eyowe towane, holi henoneta erekosetene, saka! Wata! Newa. Honalowatako ataolo eraokwa, holi ereyali Ayawanere tekwa, onedayowa erowa dane, takwanaha towane, hetikwaneta atahanatama yakoli enowana, aheranatama yakoli, wayoakataha honalowatako, araokota, koo! Enetane erekone newaha, eyowane alatane dane, Ayawanere talase taolo alataha kolowali ehe tana. Enereyali onedayoa Ayawanere erowa dane, holi deñoa owiro nawe denatama, akowalanaha enowana holi owana tohetama, akoyonatama ekiakwaha, kaweyakenatama enetane heye akwalinatamane, hala newa enetane, enanetatala laka newa, awahanatamane dane, wayonatamane awalolonatamane, hala newa kalitakase nane newe enetane detama ahotahanatamane akwalana enowana owiro ehe aokowetama , holi owana yakoli Ayawanere hatalase alikiatawa, hoyakakota aokone erekotewane , awetaho etaolo aokwa enaetaha, tohenatama yakoli aheranatamane yakoli, haita enetane erekotamane, wayoakanha eholita hoxekota ataolo, holidema Lololawalo tekotã ereñoa Ayawanere deñoa, one kenekwadewatoa ehenokota toane Ayawanere towane haweñawali toli nakota erowatowane, holi deñoa eneraneto naha towaneha, haxineha emakayowa tekwanaha exeneto naha tikalita tekwaha ereñoa, holi deratiwa ewalila ehe taeta kasalila ehe taeta , holi ehola totokoli akasenene, hekiase nowayate hetonawe tekwanaha honeta heyerane kakwa, heyeta kakwa kali, holidewa kase kaliyane, dena hanetonawe deñoa, enotowata, waikwa edoata kali, hatiwaha dosako honohera kakwa atalala wenekwatoweha, holi dosako kadene, eneto dewatiro amoyo ahotali enaeta, holi heye takwala terene kali, holi enetonaha atahanane, eheta hohona nodene hali okoyo, mahia denawe dewa wala hetonawe tekoha honeta hali okoyo, eheta hehe edane herekotita kali, hamahokwanalotita hetonawe kali, holidewa hetonawe tekwaha honeta, holi enetonawe axikedowanaha enowa, holi ako daholosehe edawali heye. Holi eneto dewatiro eyaokwa eheyeta enowana, hawawali eyeta enowana, eyerane, derohi nawe kali, holidewa eyaokwa tanita, haita tekwakoha ekiakwa nane, holidewa derati wawañali ali tanita, enakeyoa Enodawalo tekwa ekiakwa nane, Holidewa maratihe eya enowana, hane Dokoweye tenehoko, ere kakwa waita nowawañalo heyota one kataheneta one alowala kataheneta, ahetese eya enowana, ere kakwa waita heyokwa aseotoa, hala hixowata heyane heto nawe ekwalali katahe newa heye, holi deñoa netokwati dewatiro awexenalota, holidema tekwaha enonita kaliyane. Okoyoli toheta mayanetowali toheta, tonowina dayowa ameratahana towane hayaneto hekwalo, hoxikia hetase alolokwa, alolokone hataxita wayowa enowana,mahia awe holi wala waliwenakase kahita, holidema eraketene one dayowa, alatamane, walakonero alolokwa kaliyane, mahiata herehere wala wayowa enowana, hayaneto hekokota alolokwa wayowa enowana kaliyane, hayaneto ehe eyotene, haneto nawe ekwalali katahi exoata dane ekakwaha hayeneto nawe hekwalo kakwa, holidema Okoyoli kayanetoweta walakonero kakwa, holidema kohase kahali wayateta, hane kadene eyakaliti ehe tana dane, holidema Ayawaliserikwa ehe tana. Lolawekwaene nato awe nowalikwali, haitala noñakani dasewa
