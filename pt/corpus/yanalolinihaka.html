Yanaloli Nihaka

Yanaloli Nihaka Ehatowinãha, enahe kakwa, eyãokwa detaixira ehe towenane, ehose ahe eyãokwa ehetanena, ewesekoholose Lolali ehe tana, exiwire kadene, hetire ehe tana, ekano kakanowiro ehe tana. Enatoli danakata manaiyowa anihiane, eyaõkwa dakaka eriyali anihiane hanetonawe heye akone, holi asema enalinali Kalaliyama asemene eyaõkwa honako exowa dane eneraka kakwa. - Kerali akotine ewayate Anaoli, tasawati aniha hawayate eyaõkwa, haini awena eyaõkwa hanetonawe heye Yanalloli, newali kakwa exowana dane, enaliali kalaliyama eyaõkwa honako Kalaliyama. _Haia newaha eheye, holidema eyaõkwa hawayate aniha yanaloli, holi menekoya takwa towane enatoli anaoli. - Halakota nowatoli daokwa anenanawe. - haita henatoli eyaõkwa aniha, Anaho, amameya akota eyaõkwa haneto nawe heye henatoli Anaho, holidema edetaita anine, henatoli Anaho, hala newa. - Heketala nowatoli aniha, nowatoli howali eheralaka noyeta eyaõkwa nowatoli owana, alawata nowatolli aniha nowe wala, noyita eyaõkwa nowatoli owana, enerolalo toli ehe noyita eyaõkwa nowatoli owana, kerali dakakali wala eyaõkwa, hatolane mahia nakwali nowatoli heye. Alikianane. - Anena nawe nowenoli herane deyowa nowani enani kehenawe nadonixa one kolisewinã kolohekoni deya deyone nowani, towaneha alaekiaxane. Akaranekwatowaha totata towanakoha - Wiya wiyone enowana newaha. Anaõli dena erinõha, Tiwaliwali kadene oloneti owase. Anaõli ixohikase akolata, dane erinõha, ehatenane dane hokwa hiyi, Tiwaliwali wayone tekwa, Anaõli ehatane ixohikase kamamani kase alatene, esokate ehe tana. - Heketani awena wexo oka, Anaõli deta manaiyowa, tiwaliwali dane erenõwaha. - dayaha tenekati ehatena wixo ixohikase kakwa. Holidema hoxakwanatã. - Tenekati ehatena wixo anena nawe. Tenekati ehatetã wixo hoka?, Tenekati dalikailatã wixo nani, enani kehenawe nalonixa, one kolisewinãkioni newa dalikatakati wixo nani. Tiwaliwali kakwa enõlinaha. - amaserakana maiha oloniti akwanixali dewa ekialina amaserana wixo, hamaraniya tekwa holidema maiha oloneti akwanixali. - Maiha nawaserakana wala tahane deyayone laka. Maihata akotewala teokaha, Anãoli tohetawa erinõha, kahesere ako ixowa hanena, "yirere" newalini holoti kakwa ekialini "hotõtõ" newalini, hatohaita aniha kakwanaha, awanewanita amehowalitane, menekwata totenaha. Anãoli hanatoli derixaka ekialina, totaha dane wayowinãha, menane anihakotane dane, owironawe waha aninã. -Tamiro nawe, wiya Anãoli wakalalowena. Holikiwanaha waha yakoli ekano katahe, hala malikwalita ehe yakoli, hakano katahenita anihane, holikadene, ekianawalotã owiro tekwa, tekwa ekano katahenitã, totenahaxa wayowiríaha dewa, tekoha yakoli, hanena erinõha, Yola heraokotã holi hiyi takoha towane, - Nokayanetonaxa hanatolo kakwalo kakwa. _Haia hekiatawaita haõka wixo hoka, wikaninãkena xikia, Anaõli xikia aninã wixo, anã, kinãli kaxata kaliyani, heketawaita hixana wehawanita hoka? -Alawaita nakahirotowana dehawanita, newa yakoli, hokatihi, asahi maiha tetalini hekinãli, xiwitãma. wetokalakohi waha watawatahi. - Nokayanetona xikia, newa heye. - Haia, newa, hekiatawata hixana wehawanitã hoka, wikiahowalina nani, eyaka nawe ololi nawe ohokakakwana wirinõwa nani, tahane hiyayone laka kiwini kaxata kaliyani, hala, hekiatawaita hixana wehawanitã, alawaita nakahirotowana dehawanita.
